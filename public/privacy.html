Hoshi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-31

Hoshi is a Go (Weiqi/Baduk) learning app. It has no server backend, no user accounts, and does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

What data the app stores

Your saved games, exercise attempts, spaced-repetition schedule, and preferences (board theme, sound, language) are stored locally on your own device, using your browser's IndexedDB and local storage. This data never leaves your device: it is not sent to any server, because the app does not have one.

Analytics and third parties

Hoshi does not use analytics, advertising, or any third-party trackers or SDKs.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts, or any other device permission.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing site data for it from your browser, removes all locally stored data. There is nothing stored anywhere else to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the update will be published at this same URL.
